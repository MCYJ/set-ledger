Privacy

Records remain on your device

Set Ledger stores exercise names and aliases, templates and plans, session dates, weight/repetition entries, set status, historical names, drafts, undo/redo, retained imported CSV originals and recovery preservation copies in app-owned device storage. App language and guide preferences are stored separately. User-created records are not automatically translated.

No developer collection service

The app has no developer-operated backend, account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or StoreKit purchase flow. It does not send workout records to RushLabs and does not integrate HealthKit, camera or microphone capture. This describes the app, not the separate services you choose in Files or sharing.

Import, export and device backups

Choosing a file through iOS Files may download it from your selected provider. The app retains its own copy of imported CSV bytes without editing the external original. CSV exports and JSON backups contain personal records; JSON backup is readable, not encrypted. The share sheet sends the selected file to your chosen destination. Providers, receiving apps and OS backups follow their own policies and your device settings.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you change/delete them or remove app data. Undo/redo and recovery copies may retain earlier information. Delete all local records targets app-owned records, drafts, history, retained originals and verified recovery copies. Pending cleanup is shown in the app. External files, previously shared copies and OS/device backups are not deleted by that action. Logical deletion does not guarantee physical erasure of storage.

Support email and this website

If you email RushLabs at june1012june@gmail.com, your address and the content you voluntarily provide are processed to answer your request. Avoid sending private records unnecessarily. These static support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process normal request data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy. The pages include no RushLabs analytics, advertising, forms or tracking scripts.
